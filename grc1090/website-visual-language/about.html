Sun & Sip is a fresh and colorful smoothie and cold-pressed juice shop dedicated to making each drink with purely simple and healthy ingredients.

Looking for convenient, healthy, and tasty drinks? Sun & Sip offers all of that and more. With an ever-growing, transparent list of recognizable ingredients, you'll never have to think twice about a smoothie run.

We believe that eating healthy shouldn't have to be complicated or boring. That's why we focus on creating refreshing drinks packed with fresh flavors and ingredients that make you feel good.

Whether you're starting your morning, grabbing a quick drink between classes, or just craving something refreshing, Sun & Sip is here to add a little sunshine to your day. There's always something delicious waiting for you!
